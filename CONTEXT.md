# Current state

**What is true right now — in short.** Your AI reads this file right after `AGENTS.md`, before it recommends anything. This is where it learns where you're heading and what's holding you back right now.

Keep it short: a couple of sentences under each heading. When something changes, **write over it.** The old version goes into [`05-decisions/decision-log.md`](05-decisions/decision-log.md) — what it was, what it is now, why.

## Where things are stuck right now

*Topic 7 — see [`TOPICS.md`](TOPICS.md)*

One of these four. Pick the one that is true **right now**:

- Too few interested people?
- There are interested people, but no sales calls come of it?
- You have clients, but you can't keep up with serving them?
- You can keep up, but the profit is small?

> **EMPTY.** Pick one and tell your AI which it is and why. It will write it down.
>
> If you can't choose: *"look at my brain and tell me where you think I'm stuck right now."* The answer is yours, not the AI's — but it has your numbers in front of it.

A brain that doesn't know this recommends content marketing when in fact you already have more clients than you can serve. A brain that knows will tell you instead what you should hand off.
