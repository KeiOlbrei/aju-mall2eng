> **EMPTY.** Just talk — this fills up fast once you start.
>
> *"Put in my brain that I tried [thing] and it didn't work, because [reason]."*

This is the topic nobody thinks to put in their brain — and it's exactly what stops your AI from suggesting something every month that you've already tried.

**But:** "didn't work" doesn't always mean "will never work". Write it down too if the thing failed at the wrong time or for the wrong reasons.
