# Decision log

What was decided, when and **why**. This is the file that makes your brain something that grows, not just a folder.

Six weeks from now you won't remember why you made a decision. This file will. And your brain reads it before it recommends anything to you — so it won't suggest again what you've already thought through and set aside.

**One entry = one decision.** Newest at the top.

---

## *(example — delete it once your own first decision lands here)*

**2026-08-13 — I built my business brain**

**Decision:** All of my business information will live in one place, on GitHub, as text files. Everything I do with AI goes through it from now on.

**Why:** Every new chat started from zero and I explained the same context over and over. The answers were mediocre, because the AI knew nothing about my business.

**What to keep in mind:** This only works if I feed the brain. A brain that isn't fed dies.

---

> **How to add entries here:**
>
> *"Put in the decision log: I decided [what], because [why]."*
>
> The AI adds the date itself.
>
> **When a decision changes**, the old entry isn't edited. A new entry goes in: what it was before, what it is now, why it changed. The current state is written over in the right brain file — the log keeps the history, the file keeps what's true now. See `AGENTS.md`, "Keep the current state and the history apart".
