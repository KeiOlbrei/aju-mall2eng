# My numbers

*Topic 1 — see [`TOPICS.md`](../TOPICS.md)*

Your numbers right now. **The goal itself — where you want to get to — lives in [`CONTEXT.md`](../CONTEXT.md).** Here are the numbers to measure it against.

**What goes here:** revenue · how many clients · how many hours a week you work · what the average client pays you · **how many clients you can actually serve at once**

---

> **EMPTY.** Tell your AI: *"Ask me for these numbers one at a time and write them into this file."* If you have invoices or a statement from your bookkeeping, show them to it — then it can work some of it out itself.
>
> **It's completely okay if you can't answer everything right now** — say "don't know" and move on. Your brain will ask again later, when it really needs it.

**That ceiling — how many clients you can handle — is a number most people have never worked out.** And it's the only thing you can measure your progress against.

Write down where you are today **before** anything changes. A starting point you remember later isn't a starting point.
