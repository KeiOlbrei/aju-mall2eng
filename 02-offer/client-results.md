# Value and results

*Topic 6 — see [`TOPICS.md`](../TOPICS.md)*

Not what you do, but what is different for the client afterwards. And the proof: your clients' success stories.

**What goes here:** what is different in the client's life or business afterwards · results and numbers, if you have them · before-and-after examples · feedback and recommendations, **in the clients' own words** · how long it usually takes

---

> **EMPTY.** Gather up client thank-you notes, feedback, before-and-after examples. Then say:
>
> *"Read these and write here what is different for the client afterwards. Use the clients' own words, don't turn them into marketing copy."*

Without this, your AI writes about what you **do** — "interior design service" instead of "the flat sold three weeks faster".
