# Pricing

*Topic 4 — see [`TOPICS.md`](../TOPICS.md)*

What prices you sell at. **Including the prices that aren't on your website.**

**What goes here:** price per service · what you give discounts on and what you don't · payment terms · what your hourly rate is when you work it out · when you last raised your prices

---

> **EMPTY.** This is the file your brain is most likely to ask for first on its own — because without it, it can't put together a proposal for you.
>
> Show it your price list and your last few invoices, then say:
>
> *"Write my prices into this file. If an invoice has a different price than the price list, tell me."*

**This file is the only place your prices live.** If you find yourself fixing the same number in two files, something is wrong.
