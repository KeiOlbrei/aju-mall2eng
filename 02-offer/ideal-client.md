# Ideal client

*Topic 2 — see [`TOPICS.md`](../TOPICS.md)*

The clients who bring you the most joy and also the most money. And — just as important — the ones you don't want to work with any more.

**What goes here:** who they are and what they do · what's wrong, in their own words · why they come to you in particular · **who you don't want to work with, and how you recognise them** · typical objections and doubts before they buy

---

> **EMPTY.** Show Claude your last few projects, client feedback and the email thread where a client hesitated before buying. Then say:
>
> *"Read these and describe my ideal client — in their words, not in marketing language. And write out separately the objections that keep coming back."*
>
> The objections are the most valuable part here: your sales texts will have to answer them later.
