# Templates — documents you make again every time

Contract, proposal, invoice, summary, supply agreement, quote. Every business has 3–5 documents that get made over and over again, a little differently each time.

**Their templates go here** — blank forms, not filled-in documents.

---

## Why this file is more useful than it looks at first

When your brain knows **which documents your business uses at all and what shape they take**, it can do the next one for you. And it can tell you when something is missing — "your contract has no deadline".

This is also where you hand over your first task.

---

## How to make a template

Take one real document you've already sent out. **Take the client's details out first** — name, personal ID number, bank account number, address. Then say:

> *"Here's a proposal I sent. Make a template from it: keep the structure and my wording, and put [square brackets] where things change. Save it in `03-processes/templates.md`."*

And if you have several:

> *"Read these three. What's always the same in them and what changes? Make one template that covers all three."*

---

## If your document contains sensitive information

Don't just hand it to AI. Two options:

1. **Delete the sensitive lines yourself** before you show the file. The fastest way if there are only one or two documents.
2. **Use an anonymiser** — a small tool that swaps out names and numbers. We'll build it together later.

Personal ID numbers, bank account numbers, home addresses and clients' private lives don't go to AI or into the brain. The structure does.
